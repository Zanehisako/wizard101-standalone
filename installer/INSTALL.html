Wizard101 Standalone

A simple home for your next adventure.

This installer adds Wizard101 Standalone to Applications. Your official Wizard101 app and existing game files stay in place.

Apple Silicon  ·  macOS 13 or later  ·  US / Steam account candidate

3.2 experimental beta: Steam sign-in was reported working by the project owner. Full gameplay qualification is pending. Keep your previous launcher for rollback; this installer replaces the standalone app in Applications.

Three steps to play

  1. 1Prepare Mac

    Open the app from Applications. If asked, complete Apple’s Rosetta prompt, then choose Check again.

  2. 2Install or reuse game

    Choose a detected game, browse to a folder containing Bin and Data, or download a fresh copy. Imports leave the original files intact.

  3. 3Sign in and Play

    Choose US or Steam. Steam uses the Wizard101 credentials created through Steam and needs the signed-in Mac Steam client. Both use the same downloaded game. Remember me saves passwords separately in Mac Keychain.

If macOS asks for approval

This beta is unsigned and not notarized. After a blocked-open alert, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, choose Open Anyway and confirm Open. Repeat for the app if needed.
