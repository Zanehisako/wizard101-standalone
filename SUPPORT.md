# Feedback and support

**[Join our Discord server →](https://discord.gg/b2KVmJ84P)**

Discord is our main contact for bug reports, setup questions and suggestions.

**Using Diagnostic Build 16? [How to save and send us your logs](README-LOGS.md).**
Save a report ZIP from Settings, then attach it to your Discord message.

1. Open the invite above in your browser or Discord app.
2. Accept the invite, then post your feedback or question in the server.

For a bug, include the app version, your Mac/macOS, **US or Steam**, and what
happened. Reproduction steps and a screenshot help.

Thank you for helping improve Wizard101 for Mac!
